📚 STUDY SMART

Study Smarter, Not Just Harder.

Practical techniques that can make your study sessions more organized and focused.

01

🎯 Set a Clear Goal

Before opening your book, decide exactly what you want to complete.

Example: "Complete Science Chapter 5."
02

⏱️ Use Focus Sessions

Divide your study time into focused sessions with short breaks.

Example: 25 minutes study + 5 minutes break.
03

🧠 Active Recall

Close your book and try to explain what you remember.

Example: Explain the lesson without looking.
04

✍️ Practice Questions

Test yourself instead of only reading your textbook.

Example: Solve 10 questions after a lesson.
05

🔄 Review Regularly

Revisit older topics periodically instead of waiting until exam time.

06

📵 Control Distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and distractions away during study sessions.
